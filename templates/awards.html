How These Awards Are Scored

Places & Top 3
Taken from each boat’s finishing place. Every angler in a boat is credited with that boat’s finish.
Money Won
$20 / $14 / $8 per boat for 1st / 2nd / 3rd, plus the big bass pot. A two-angler boat splits its winnings 50/50. Totals match each tournament’s payout page, rollovers included.
Fish & Total Weight
Boat totals. A shared bag can’t be split between partners, so both anglers are credited the full bag.
Big Stringer & Big Bass
Single-event records. Big bass must clear 5.00 lbs and be boated by a team with at least one member; otherwise the pot rolls to the next tournament.
By Angler vs. By Team
Partners re-pair between events, so the angler boards show a full season while the team boards show how each exact pairing did together.
Who Appears
Angler boards list club members. Team boards list any boat with at least one member, guest partners included.
